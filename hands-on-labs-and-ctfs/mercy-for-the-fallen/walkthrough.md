# Walkthrough — Mercy For The Fallen (Spoiler-Free)

⚠️ This walkthrough explains the investigation methodology without revealing solutions or flags.

---

## Challenge Design Philosophy

The challenge follows a layered investigation model where each discovery validates the participant’s analytical thinking.

There is intentionally no explicit starting instruction.

Participants must rely on observation and structured enumeration.

---

## Phase 1 — Initial Exploration

Competitors begin with an archive containing multiple unfamiliar artifacts.

Key objective:
- Identify meaningful files
- Verify real file types
- Avoid trusting filenames alone

---

## Phase 2 — Artifact Analysis

One object behaves as a container rather than a standard file.

Participants must recognize hidden data embedded within another format.

This stage introduces forensic inspection concepts.

---

## Phase 3 — Signal Investigation

Recovered artifacts contain concealed information encoded within media data.

Success depends on understanding how information may exist beyond visible content.

---

## Phase 4 — Logical Progression

Discovered information acts as proof of investigation progress.

Participants must interpret findings rather than rely purely on automated tools.

---

## Phase 5 — Verification Mechanism

A validation stage confirms whether earlier discoveries were correctly understood.

This simulates real-world authentication and access control systems.

---

## Phase 6 — Final Revelation

The final stage represents completion of the investigative journey.

Only structured reasoning and patience lead to success.

---

## Learning Outcomes

- Think like an attacker
- Perform structured enumeration
- Understand layered data hiding concepts
- Combine technical skill with analytical reasoning
